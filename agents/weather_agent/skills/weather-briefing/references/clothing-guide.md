# Clothing guide

Pick the band that contains today's **high** temperature in °C.

| High (°C) | Band | Wear |
|---|---|---|
| 25 and above | hot | light, breathable clothes such as shorts and a t-shirt, plus sunscreen |
| 18 to 24 | warm | a t-shirt or light shirt, with a light layer for the evening if the low is under 15°C |
| 10 to 17 | mild | a light jacket or sweater over a long-sleeve shirt |
| 3 to 9 | cold | a warm coat and long trousers, with a hat or scarf if it is windy |
| below 3 | freezing | a heavy winter coat, hat, gloves and scarf |

# Rain rule

Pick the rule for today's **chance of precipitation**.

| Chance | Rain line |
|---|---|
| 60% or more | bring an umbrella or a rain jacket; rain is likely |
| 30% to 59% | an umbrella is worth having in your bag |
| under 30% | no umbrella needed |

# Wind note

If the current wind is **20 km/h or more**, add: `Wind: it is breezy, so a windproof outer layer helps.`
